Method

Evidence before inference.

TradeStatus monitors public status pages, maintenance schedules and operational bulletins. Every item links to the underlying source and records when it was last checked.

Operational is reserved for systems with an explicit health signal. No active advisory means the monitored public bulletin contains no current event; it is not treated as proof that every component is working.

Official government and port-authority sources are preferred. Secondary operational sources are clearly labelled when an authoritative machine-readable feed is not publicly available.
